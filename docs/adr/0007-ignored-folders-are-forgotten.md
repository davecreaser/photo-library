# Photos in a newly ignored folder are forgotten, not hidden

When the user ignores a folder, the app forgets its photos entirely (records, faces, names, tags, cached thumbnails and face crops) instead of hiding them. Ignoring is mostly used for privacy, and a hidden-but-kept record of private photos (including face data) would defeat that. The cost: un-ignoring brings the photos back without their earlier names and tags. (Photos that merely go missing, e.g. on an unplugged drive, are hidden and remembered instead.)
